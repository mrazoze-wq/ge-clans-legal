Temp Room - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Temp Room, a Discord temporary voice channel management bot, processes and stores information.

2. Information We Process

Temp Room processes Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, voice channel events, and information needed to manage temporary voice rooms.

It may store room ownership, assigned administrators, room settings, permissions, creation timestamps, server configuration, and related operational data.

3. Message Content

Temp Room processes message content when necessary to recognize and execute text-based commands and their arguments.

The application is not designed to maintain a permanent archive of ordinary chat messages.

4. How Data Is Used

Information is used to create and manage temporary voice channels, enforce permissions, process commands, configure rooms, and maintain application functionality and security.

5. Data Storage

Operational configuration and room management data may be stored in a database. Access to stored data is restricted to authorized application operations and administrators.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Operational data is retained as necessary to provide the service. Temporary room records may be removed during automated cleanup or administrative operations.

Users may request deletion of personal data associated with their Discord account by contacting the application owner through Discord.

7. Data Sharing

Temp Room does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting and database providers where necessary to operate the service, or disclosed when legally required.

8. Artificial Intelligence

Temp Room does not use message content to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards are used to protect operational data against unauthorized access, modification, and disclosure.

10. Discord Platform

Temp Room operates through Discord. Discord's own processing of information is governed by Discord's applicable privacy policies.

11. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when application features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Temp Room application owner through Discord.
