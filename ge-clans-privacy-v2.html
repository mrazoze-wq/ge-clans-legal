GE Clans - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GE Clans processes Discord information to provide clan management, membership, activity tracking, and related server administration features.

1. Introduction

GE Clans ("the Bot") is a Discord application that provides clan and group management functionality within Discord servers.

This policy explains the categories of information processed by the Bot, why that information is needed, how it may be stored, and how users can request assistance with their information.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled by server administrators, the Bot may process:

  • Discord user and server identifiers.
  • Discord role and channel identifiers.
  • Clan names, ownership, and membership.
  • Clan administrator and permission settings.
  • Clan applications and invitations.
  • Clan creation and configuration records.
  • Text and voice participation metrics.
  • Clan activity points and leaderboard data.
  • Administrative settings and command data.
  • Information needed to manage deleted or restorable clans, where supported.

The Bot processes information relevant to its configured features and Discord permissions.

3. Message Content

GE Clans uses message content to recognize and process prefix-based commands, including command names and arguments.

The Bot may also process messages submitted during clan creation and renaming workflows to obtain and validate clan names.

Message events may be used to calculate clan participation points and activity statistics without requiring permanent storage of ordinary message text.

The Bot is not designed to maintain a permanent archive of general Discord conversations.

4. Clan and Membership Data

The Bot processes clan ownership, membership, invitations, applications, and administrative assignments to manage clan access and permissions.

Authorized clan administrators may be able to view or manage relevant clan membership information through Discord commands and interactive panels.

5. Activity Tracking

GE Clans may calculate activity points based on eligible message events and participation in voice channels.

This information supports clan rankings, leaderboards, and activity statistics.

Activity tracking is intended to measure participation within the Bot's clan management features rather than to create a general history of private conversations.

6. Voice Channel Information

The Bot may process voice channel join, leave, and movement events to manage clan voice permissions and calculate participation statistics.

Voice channel event processing does not itself require recording or analyzing spoken audio.

7. How Information Is Used

Information is processed for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing clans.
  • Managing members and clan ownership.
  • Configuring clan roles and channels.
  • Processing applications and invitations.
  • Calculating activity points and rankings.
  • Processing authorized commands.
  • Enforcing permissions and restrictions.
  • Maintaining service reliability and security.

8. Data Storage

GE Clans may store operational information in databases or files hosted outside Discord.

Stored information may include clan configuration, Discord identifiers, membership records, activity points, and other data needed for the Bot's functionality.

Access to operational data is restricted according to the Bot's technical and administrative controls.

9. Data Retention

Operational information may be retained while it is needed to provide clan management functionality.

Some information may remain temporarily available in deletion or restoration workflows, where those features are supported.

Retention may also depend on operational requirements, applicable law, and legitimate security needs.

10. Data Deletion Requests

Users may contact the GE Clans operator through Discord to request information about stored personal data or to request its deletion.

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable requirements and the technical operation of the service.

Some clan information may be shared operational data associated with other members or server administration.

11. Data Sharing

GE Clans does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers necessary to operate the Bot, or disclosed when required by law, applicable platform requirements, or legitimate security obligations.

Certain clan information may be visible to authorized members or administrators through the Bot's normal features.

12. Artificial Intelligence

GE Clans does not use Discord message content to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

13. Security

Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss of stored information.

No system can guarantee absolute security.

14. Discord Platform

GE Clans operates using the Discord API and Discord's platform services.

Discord's independent processing of information is governed by its own privacy policies and terms.

Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy

15. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the Bot's functionality, information processing, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published with a revised effective date.

16. Contact

For privacy questions, data inquiries, or deletion requests, users may contact the GE Clans bot operator through Discord.
