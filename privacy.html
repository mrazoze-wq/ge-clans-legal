GE Clans - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GE Clans ("the Bot") collects, processes, stores, and uses information when providing clan and group management features within Discord servers.

2. Information We Process

GE Clans may process information provided through the Discord API, including Discord user IDs, server IDs, role IDs, channel IDs, and other information necessary to identify users, servers, roles, and permissions.

The Bot may also store information required for its clan and group management features, such as group membership, group ownership, administrator assignments, applications, invitations, configuration settings, and other related operational data.

3. Message Content

GE Clans may process message content when necessary to recognize and execute prefix-based commands and their arguments. Message content is not stored as a permanent message history by the Bot.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to provide and maintain GE Clans features, process commands, manage groups and memberships, enforce permissions, prevent unauthorized actions, and maintain the operation and security of the Bot.

5. Data Storage

Certain operational data may be stored off-platform in a database for as long as it is required to provide the Bot's features or until it is deleted. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored data.

6. Data Sharing

GE Clans does not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable law, protect the service and its users, or meet Discord platform requirements.

7. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Message content processed by GE Clans is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Operational data may remain stored while it is required for the Bot's functionality. Users may request deletion of stored data associated with their Discord account by contacting the GE Clans bot owner directly through Discord.

9. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

10. Discord

GE Clans operates through the Discord platform. Discord's own collection and processing of information is governed by Discord's applicable terms and privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when GE Clans features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning stored data, users may contact the GE Clans bot owner directly through Discord.
