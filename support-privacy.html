Support Bot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Support Bot processes information to provide voice support management features within Discord servers.

2. Information We Process

Support Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, role IDs, channel IDs, voice channel events, support requests, and configuration information.

The bot may also process the online or offline presence status of support team members to display support availability.

3. Message Content

Support Bot processes message content when necessary to recognize and execute prefix-based administrative and owner commands.

The bot is not designed to maintain a permanent archive of ordinary chat messages.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to manage support queues, notify authorized support staff, process support requests, manage voice channel interactions, enforce permissions, and maintain bot functionality and security.

5. Data Storage

Support Bot may store operational settings, support configuration, and information necessary to maintain support workflows.

Stored information is protected using reasonable technical and administrative safeguards.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Operational information is retained only as necessary to provide the bot's functionality. Temporary support session information may be removed when sessions end or during cleanup.

Users may request deletion of stored personal data associated with their Discord account by contacting the bot operator.

7. Data Sharing

Support Bot does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting providers where necessary to operate the service, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

8. Artificial Intelligence

Support Bot does not use message content to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

9. Security

Reasonable security measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

10. Discord Platform

Support Bot operates through Discord. Discord's own processing of information is governed by Discord's applicable privacy policies.

11. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Support Bot operator through Discord.
